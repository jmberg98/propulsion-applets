Gridded Ion Engine III: Neutralization

Electric Propulsion

The ion jet emitted by the accelerating grids must be neutralized for the device to operate as desired. Like mass and energy, charge is conserved on our spacecraft. If we expel a large amount of positive charge from the spacecraft, the spacecraft will be left with a large negative charge. Not only does this pose a risk to on-board electronics, but the negatively charged spacecraft attracts the positively charged exhaust jet, canceling thrust $F_N$.1,3

Color key thrust ion electron

If we instead neutralize the beam by shooting electrons at it with a neutralizer cathode, we get a quasineutral plasma jet.2,4,5 The exhaust will then be a beam consisting of positive and negative charges, but is, on average, electrically neutral.
